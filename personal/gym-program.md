# Eddy's Personal Gym Program

## Goal
Bodybuilding-focused 5-day training split designed to develop every major muscle group while keeping Thursday and Friday as full recovery days.

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Training | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Chest + Back | Upper-body mass |
| Tuesday | Quads + Calves | Quad-dominant legs |
| Wednesday | Shoulders + Arms | Delts, biceps, triceps |
| Thursday | REST | Recovery |
| Friday | REST | Recovery |
| Saturday | Hamstrings + Glutes | Posterior chain |
| Sunday | Upper Body | Second chest/back/delt/arm stimulus |

## Monday — Chest + Back
Alternate pushing and pulling movements.

1. Incline Press — 3 × 5–6
2. Chest-Supported Row — 3 × 5–6
3. Machine Chest Press — 3 × 8–12
4. Lat Pulldown — 3 × 8–12
5. Cable Fly — 3 × 10–15
6. Seated/Cable Row — 3 × 10–15

**Volume:** 9 working sets chest + 9 working sets back.

## Tuesday — Quads + Calves

1. Hack Squat — 3 × 5–6
2. Leg Press — 3 × 8–12
3. Leg Extension — 3 × 10–15
4. Bulgarian Split Squat — 3 × 8–12
5. Standing Calf Raise — 4 × 8–12
6. Seated Calf Raise — 3 × 12–15

## Wednesday — Shoulders + Arms

1. Machine or Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 3 × 5–6
2. Cable Lateral Raise — 3 × 10–15
3. Machine Lateral Raise — 3 × 10–15
4. Rear-Delt Fly — 3 × 12–15
5. EZ-Bar Curl — 3 × 8–12
6. Incline Curl — 3 × 10–12
7. Triceps Pressdown — 3 × 8–12
8. Overhead Triceps Extension — 3 × 10–12

## Thursday — REST
Full recovery day.

## Friday — REST
Full recovery day.

## Saturday — Hamstrings + Glutes

1. Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 5–6
2. Seated Leg Curl — 3 × 8–12
3. Hip Thrust — 3 × 8–12
4. Lying Leg Curl — 3 × 10–15
5. Glute-Focused Back Extension — 3 × 10–15
6. Calves — 3 × 10–15

## Sunday — Upper Body
Second weekly stimulus without repeating Monday's heavy workload.

1. Incline Machine Press — 3 × 8–12
2. Neutral-Grip Pulldown — 3 × 8–12
3. Chest Press — 3 × 8–12
4. Chest-Supported Row — 3 × 8–12
5. Lateral Raise — 3 × 12–15
6. Rear-Delt Fly — 3 × 12–15
7. Biceps — 2 × 10–15
8. Triceps — 2 × 10–15

## Training Approach

- Use one heavier 5–6 rep movement for the major muscle groups, followed by bodybuilding work primarily in the 8–15 rep range.
- Prioritize controlled technique and progressive overload.
- Most working sets should finish with roughly 1–2 good reps in reserve; selected final sets can be pushed harder.
- Rest about 2–3+ minutes on heavy 5–6 rep movements and about 1.5–2 minutes on moderate-rep hypertrophy work.
- Avoid the high-row variation that repeatedly causes cramping; use a comfortable chest-supported row or pulldown variation instead.
- Thursday and Friday remain full lifting rest days.
